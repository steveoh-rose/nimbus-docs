## Features

The DatePicker is a custom component following the [DatePicker Aria Pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/examples/datepicker-dialog/).
It has the following features:

- Dates & times – Choose dates, times, or both with configurable precision.
- International – 13+ calendar systems, locale formats, number systems, RTL, and hour cycles.
- Time zone aware – Handles daylight saving and time zone rules.
- Accessible – Keyboard navigation, screen reader support, and a calendar popover.
- Touch friendly – Optimized for mobile keyboards and touch screen readers.
- Validation – Works with forms, supports required/min/max, disabled dates, and custom rules.
- Customizable – Full control over DOM and styling.
