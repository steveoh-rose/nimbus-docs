---
title: Shadows
description: Elevation levels used to separate surfaces from the background.
---

<ComponentPreview name="shadow-scale" />

Shadows communicate elevation — how far a surface sits above the page. Use
the smallest shadow that reads clearly: cards typically need only
`shadow-sm`, while overlays like dialogs and popovers use `shadow-lg` or
`shadow-xl` to read as detached from the page.
