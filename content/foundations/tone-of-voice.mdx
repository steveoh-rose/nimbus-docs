Console Connect takes traditionally lengthy, complex processes and makes them as simple as possible. It is important this is reflected in our tone of voice, the manner in which we talk to our users.

With a work-smarter-not-harder attitude, our tone is fairly relaxed and friendly, yet always to the point:

<Note title="A good test">What would a helpful human say?</Note>

## Global audience

First and foremost, we must remember that we have a truly global audience. This increases our need to speak to our users clearly, and not use colloquialisms and idioms that an international audience may not understand.

### Humour, wit and sarcasm

Our global audience is also an important consideration when it comes to humour, and is why Console Connect doesn't try to be funny. It is absolutely possible to be light-hearted in our delivery, but avoid trying to be witty or sarcastic, for the risk of being "lost in translation".

## Tone words

It's important to understand what tone _isn't_ in order to get it right. Tone may vary depending on the type of message. Some elements may be dialled up or down for different purposes.

Anything written for Console Connect should be based on building trust and being informative in a friendly and respectful manner.

- Our tone is mostly **matter-of-fact**, often **conversational**, and always **clear and succinct**.
- Our tone is **not formal**, **not irreverent**, and **not sarcastic**.

It's a good idea to consider different word combinations to evaluate tone. For example: a touch of wit but not funny; professional but not overly formal; matter-of-fact but not dry.

Whilst we want to be clear, concise and always respectful, we also want to present a little personality without being totally dry. We do this through subtle means, and treat tone as something you dial up and down depending on the circumstance.
